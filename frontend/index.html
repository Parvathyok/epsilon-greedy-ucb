How to read this comparison

Pseudo-regret adds the difference between the best available mean reward and the mean of each chosen action. It measures the expected cost of the decisions, without extra reward-draw noise. Table intervals are approximate 95% Monte Carlo intervals across runs.

All policies sample each action once and use sample-mean updates. The saved reversal study also includes a constant-step ε-greedy diagnostic. The same potential rewards are shared across policies; unchosen rewards remain hidden from the learners.
